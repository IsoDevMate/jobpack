# Gold cover letter shape (Times, plain paragraphs, no dashes with AI smell)

Header block (top-left, bold name, then contact lines):
    YOUR NAME
    your phone
    your email
    Your city

Date, then the address block:
    Graduate Programme Team / Hiring Team
    <Company>
    <City>

Salutation:  Dear Hiring Team,

Para 1 - who I am + interest + hard eligibility hook:
    I am applying for the <exact JD title> at <Company>. I hold <degree from
    facts.json>, so <one eligibility fact you can prove>, and I am interested
    in this role because <one sentence connecting their problem to work you
    already do>.

Para 2 - the strongest proof:
    Ship-facing sentence with a name and an outcome. "I shipped a live <thing>
    inside <project>: <what you built>, <how measured>, <what fails it guards
    against>." One paragraph, no tool laundry list, no "I have experience with".

Para 3 - the second proof:
    A different job from the CV with a concrete outcome. "At <employer> I
    <action verb> <thing> so <business consequence>." Only employers that are
    on the CV. Never "I do not have X".

Para 4 - availability, no thank-you yet:
    "I am based in <city> and available to start <when>. I close by naming the
    employer's real values in one honest sentence."

Auto-close (pdf_letter.py appends):
    Thank you for your time. I look forward to your response.
    Yours sincerely,
    <name>, <phone>, <email>

Hard rules:
- Never "I want this role because" (use "I am interested in this role because").
- Never "Your role asks for X. That matches Y." (formula; flagged by gates).
- Never em-dash (—) or en-dash (–). ASCII hyphen only.
- Never "I do not have" / "I am not a" / any gap sentence.
- Max 2 uses of "I work with / I have experience / I have worked".
- Name the same employers that appear on the attached CV. No new ones, no omissions of the pages it cites.
